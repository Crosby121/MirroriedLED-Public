# Website image assets

The hero and four product cards use optimized WebP images. The infinity mirror image is reused for the hero and its product card. Visible captions distinguish design concepts from the LED panel demonstration photo.

## Provenance

- `infinity-mirror.webp`, `stadium-model.webp`, `address-sign.webp`: created with the built-in imagegen tool as product design concepts; no claim of completed fabrication or installation.
- `led-display.webp`: owner-supplied RGB LED panel demonstration photograph, converted to WebP without embedded camera metadata.

All images are project-owned local assets; the website has no external image hotlinks. HTML references carry content-hash versions to avoid stale browser caches. Update the matching `?v=` hash whenever an asset changes.

## Generation prompts

### infinity-mirror

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: website hero and product-card image for Mirroried LED, a custom infinity-mirror fabrication business. Create a premium photorealistic design-concept rendering of one square custom infinity mirror, approximately 24 by 24 inches and four inches deep, with a slim black wood frame and a two-way mirrored front. Show a physically plausible deep infinity tunnel of softly diffused turquoise, electric-blue and subtle violet perimeter lights, repeated progressively smaller mirror reflections into the center, and delicate frosted abstract wave engraving on the rear mirror, with smooth luminous etching rather than exposed LED dots in the engraved artwork. Place the single finished concept object on a dark studio plinth against a restrained charcoal backdrop. Slight three-quarter view, full square mirror and its edges visible, centered within a landscape 3:2 composition with generous margins so it also crops cleanly into a square hero. Polished product photography style, realistic glass, restrained bloom, crisp craftsmanship details. No people, no text, no logos, no watermark, no collage, no extra mirrors, no impossible glowing frame shapes. This is a design concept rather than documentary evidence of a completed product.

### stadium-model

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: landscape website product-card image for Mirroried LED's custom laser-fabricated stadium builds. Create a premium photorealistic design-concept rendering of one small tabletop baseball stadium model constructed from layered laser-cut Baltic birch plywood. Show clearly visible stacked birch layers, tidy warm laser-darkened edges, detailed seating terraces, the baseball diamond and modest grass-green playing field, and accurately assembled miniature structural elements. The object must read as a handcrafted miniature wooden stadium, not a full-size stadium, plastic toy or a flat map. Place it on a dark studio plinth against a restrained charcoal background, with soft warm light revealing the wood texture and slight cool blue rim lighting to match an LED fabrication brand. Elevated three-quarter camera angle, entire model comfortably visible with clear margins, landscape 3:2 composition suitable for a product card. No people, no league/team logos, no readable lettering, no watermark, no collage, no tools around it. This is a design concept, not a photograph of a confirmed completed build.

### address-sign

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: landscape website product-card image for Mirroried LED's address signs and mailboxes. Create a premium photorealistic design-concept rendering of a modern rectangular black wall-mounted mailbox combined with an illuminated infinity-mirror address panel directly beneath it as one coherent unit. The upper mailbox is a squared rectangular box with a practical flip-up top lid, closed in this image. The lower rectangular mirror panel displays the exact large frosted glowing white numerals "128", with a physically plausible softly diffused turquoise-blue perimeter infinity tunnel receding behind the numbers. Realistic black powder-coated metal, mirrored glass and clean fabrication details, about four inches deep. Mount the entire unit on a quiet charcoal exterior wall beside a small unobtrusive edge of a doorway, close framing but full product visible with generous margins, landscape 3:2 composition. Soft evening architectural lighting, restrained bloom, no people, no street name, no extra text, no logos, no watermark, no curved or round mailbox, no unrelated props. This is a custom design concept, not a photograph of a verified installed product.
